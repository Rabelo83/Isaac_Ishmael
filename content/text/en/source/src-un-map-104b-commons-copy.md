**The map.** The scan shows the complete sheet. Its titles are printed in English and French. They read, in English, "City of Jerusalem: Boundaries Proposed by the Ad Hoc Committee on the Palestinian Question" and, in French, "Ville de Jérusalem: Limites proposées par la Commission ad hoc chargée de la question palestinienne". The sheet also has a legend, scale bars and the footer "Map No. 104 (b), United Nations, November 1947". These quotations are transcriptions with capitalization and accents normalized. The sheet has no coordinate grid. The date shown on this page is the map's printed issue date, not the date the copy was uploaded.

**The copy.** Third-party hosting does not turn this primary map into secondary scholarship, and it does not make the copy official.
