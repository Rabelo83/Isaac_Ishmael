Editorial explanation, reviewed on 11 October 2026 by Codex (AI editorial review); not independently reviewed by a person. **Category: proposed arrangement.** The documentary basis is a General Assembly resolution; this page does not describe an implemented boundary or a court judgment.

Resolution 181 (II), dated 29 November 1947, proposed Jerusalem as a *corpus separatum*: a separate entity under a special international regime administered by the United Nations, with the Trusteeship Council acting as administering authority. [Part III A, PDF page 10](https://unispal.un.org/pdfs/7F0AF2BD897689B785256C330061D253.pdf#page=10) :cite[cit-res181-part-iii-a]

The proposed area included the municipality and surrounding places: Abu Dis eastward, Bethlehem southward, Ein Karim including built-up Motsa westward, and Shu'fat northward. The text refers to Annex B. [Part III B, PDF page 10](https://unispal.un.org/pdfs/7F0AF2BD897689B785256C330061D253.pdf#page=10) :cite[cit-res181-part-iii-b]
