# Project charter

## Purpose

Enable independent judgment about claims concerning the land encompassing present-day Israel, the West Bank, East Jerusalem, and Gaza through inspectable historical evidence, applicable legal authorities, and documented proposals. Begin with the earliest available evidence and examine the Abraham, Isaac, and Ishmael traditions in their textual and historical contexts. Continue through the present, with a stated research cutoff for each release.

The trial analogy supplies discipline: clear questions, evidence, objections, and reasoned findings. The website is an educational research resource, not a tribunal. No project-authored verdict on who deserves the land is a required deliverable.

## Agreed scope

- English and Spanish public content, suitable for personal study and sharing in discussions.
- Genealogical traditions and later ancestry claims, distinguished from independently corroborated descent.
- Historical populations, migration, residence, institutions, political control, landholding, and displacement.
- Distinct questions of sovereignty, self-determination, occupation, individual property, residence, citizenship, refugee claims, and access to religious sites.
- Legal instruments and decisions directly relevant to these questions, followed by carefully justified comparative cases.
- Historical and contemporary settlement proposals from parties, mediators, international bodies, and relevant civil-society initiatives.
- Antisemitism and other persecution where necessary to explain documented developments; these are contextual subjects, not the organizing thesis.
- Clickable source links and precise locators so readers can conduct independent research.

## Research boundaries

Do not assume modern Jews, Arabs, Palestinians, Israelis, Christians, or Muslims form two biologically traceable branches. Examine each ancestry claim on its evidence and distinguish ethnicity, religion, nationality, language, and citizenship. Do not treat descent, antiquity, religious attachment, political control, or private ownership as interchangeable evidence.

Historical documentation requires source criticism. Legal application requires interpretation. Attribute judicial reasoning and scholarly disagreement; label any project synthesis explicitly. Avoid promising an interpretation-free account or certainty the evidence cannot sustain.

No notebook, user accounts, public comments, automated debate bot, popularity voting, or numerical score for which people have the strongest rights is planned.

## Governance

Codex maintains scope, research standards, requirements, tasks, review criteria, and handoff documents. Claude Code selects and implements an appropriate technical solution within those requirements and reports validation results. The project owner decides product direction. Neither agent may invent sources, quotations, findings, or source-review completion.

The scope above reflects the conversation. Detailed delivery counts and interface choices in the accompanying documents are initial planning proposals, adjustable as research reveals constraints.

## Success

A reader can start from a question, follow the history, inspect a map with its date and limitations, open the supporting sources, distinguish competing claims from adjudicated findings, compare proposals, and share the same record in either language.

## Resumen en español

El objetivo es estudiar la historia y las distintas clases de derechos sobre el territorio, sin imponer una conclusión. La investigación separará las tradiciones genealógicas, los hechos documentados, los argumentos de las partes y las decisiones jurídicas. También comparará propuestas de solución y sus respuestas documentadas. Cada afirmación publicada tendrá fuentes accesibles mediante enlaces. Codex dirigirá la planificación y la revisión; Claude Code realizará la programación. No se incluirá un cuaderno personal.
