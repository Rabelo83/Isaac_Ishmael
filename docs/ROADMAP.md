## Publication checks

- Every material factual assertion has an inspected supporting citation with a clickable link and locator where available.
- Source access restrictions, disputed readings, and important counterevidence are visible.
- Every legal finding states the issuing body, procedural stage, scope, and exact reference.
- Every comparison includes limits, and every proposal identifies its version and sourced responses.
- Maps have dates, legends, source provenance, and reuse rights.
- English and Spanish entries match in meaning and uncertainty; unpublished translations do not masquerade as reviewed content.
- No draft fixtures, secrets, unlicensed source reproductions, or invented research enter the public build.
- Internal links, IDs, citation references, routing under the repository path, direct page refresh, build output, and mobile/keyboard use have been checked.
- The release states its research cutoff, coverage gaps, and correction history.

## Decisions and open choices

Confirmed: bilingual site; all named territories; earliest evidence through the present; precedents and legal standards; proposals from parties and intermediaries; direct source links; GitHub Pages; no notebook; Codex manages and Claude programs.

Still open: public-facing title/subtitle, visual style, first expanded historical period, framework selection, and licensing for original project content. These do not block initial research. No permission to relicense third-party sources is implied.
